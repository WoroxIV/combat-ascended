Combat Ascended

A deeper melee combat system for any weapon mod: combos, parries, dashes, dual wielding and two-handed weapons.

Combat Ascended is the combat system of Better Weaponry, now in its own mod so that it works with any weapon, from vanilla swords to other weapon mods.

Features

  • Combos chained by timing your hits.
  • Parries and perfect parries, even with vanilla swords.
  • Dashes to dodge and close the distance.
  • Dual wielding for light weapons, and two-handed heavy weapons.
  • Armor penetration and on-hit effects that depend on the weapon.
  • About 50 weapon archetypes (daggers, katanas, spears, hammers...). Weapons from other mods are detected automatically, from the files the mod ships and then from the item name.
  • An in-game editor to tune every weapon, and tutorials to learn the moves.
  • Modpacks add or change a weapon with a three-line JSON file. /combatascended scan lists what was detected.

Compatibility

  • Better Weaponry: all its weapons are recognized, and they behave exactly as before the split. Your combat settings from Better Weaponry 2.0 are imported automatically.
  • Better Combat: Better Combat keeps reach, sweeping, two-handed and dual wielding. Everything else in Combat Ascended keeps working.
  • Epic Fight: not compatible. Combat Ascended refuses to start with it, since both replace the whole combat system.
  • Available for NeoForge and Fabric 1.21.1, and Forge and Fabric 1.20.1.
