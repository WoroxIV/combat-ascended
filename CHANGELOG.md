## 1.0.0

First version, split from Better Weaponry 2.0.

- Combos, parries, perfect parries, dashes, dual wielding, two-handed weapons, armor penetration, on-hit effects, the in-game editor and the tutorials, now for any weapon mod.
- About 50 weapon archetypes, automatic detection from the files a mod ships, then from the item name.
- Mods and modpacks add a weapon with a three-line JSON file. New `/combatreforged scan` command and a Detected weapons page in the editor.
- The six vanilla swords can parry.
- With Better Combat, Better Combat handles reach, sweep, two-handed weapons and dual wielding, and everything else keeps working.
- Refuses to start with Epic Fight.
- Imports the combat settings of Better Weaponry 2.0.
